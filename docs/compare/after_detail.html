Automatic Wraparound Labeling Machine for Square & Round Bottles


This high-precision automatic labeling machine is engineered for reliable, high-speed wraparound labeling of square, rectangular, and round containers — ideal for PET bottles, glass bottles, jars, cans, and rigid packaging used across food, beverage, cosmetic, and household product industries.

Key Applications & Industry Use Cases



  • Food Packaging: Sauce jars, seasoning containers, snack pouches, food-grade PET bottles

  • Beverage Packaging: Water bottles, juice bottles, carbonated drink bottles, glass beverage containers

  • Cosmetic Packaging: Lotion bottles, cream jars, skincare containers, aluminum tubes

  • Household Products: Detergent bottles, cleaning solution containers, disinfectant dispensers

  • General Packaging: Cartons, rigid boxes, metal cans, flexible pouches, and composite containers

Design & Operational Advantages



  • Multi-Configuration Flexibility: One machine handles diverse container geometries — including square PET bottles requiring precise corner-wrap alignment and cylindrical bottles needing consistent circumferential application.

  • Quick Size Changeover: Adjustable conveyor width, height, and guide mechanisms enable rapid adaptation to varying container dimensions without tooling changes.

  • User-Centric Operation: Touchscreen interface simplifies setup, real-time monitoring, and fault diagnostics — minimizing operator training time and downtime.

  • Production-Line Ready: Modular design allows seamless integration into automated packaging lines or standalone operation based on facility requirements.

  • Customization Support: Configurable options include coding devices (inkjet or thermal transfer), extended conveyor modules, custom infeed/outfeed structures, and label applicator adaptations per container profile.
